# Wireless AP Troubleshooting

1. Identify affected venue zone, SSID, client count, and whether the issue is authentication, coverage, or throughput.
2. Check AP online state, controller adoption, channel utilization, client load, retries, and recent radio/config changes.
3. Compare nearby APs and neighboring zones to determine whether impact is localized or shared.
4. Validate VLAN, DHCP, DNS, and upstream reachability for the affected SSID; check authentication service health for 802.1X issues.
5. Coordinate with event operations before changing channel, power, SSID, or AP placement during doors or live play.
6. Capture affected APs, client impact, tests, vendor/controller case, and restoration confirmation in the incident record.
