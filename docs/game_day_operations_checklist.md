# Game-Day Operations Checklist

## Before doors
- Confirm primary and backup circuit reachability, latency, and packet loss.
- Review core/distribution switch uplinks, interface errors, VLAN paths, and spanning-tree state.
- Confirm wireless AP/controller health and expected guest, staff, and production SSIDs.
- Verify ticketing, point-of-sale, broadcast, access-control, and directory services.
- Confirm latest backup completion and recovery point objective.
- Review open incidents, provider tickets, escalation contacts, and stakeholder update owners.

## During event
- Watch venue-wide alerts and business service impact; correlate with event operations reports.
- Record incident decisions and timestamps; engage providers early for circuit degradation.
- Coordinate potentially disruptive network changes with the event lead.
- Send plain-language updates at the agreed cadence.

## After event / shift change
- Confirm services returned to expected baseline.
- Capture unresolved issues, vendor next steps, and day-shift owners in a saved handoff.
